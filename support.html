Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Email support@caretrace.uk with the subject "Password Reset" and we'll reset your account within 24 hours.

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Email support@caretrace.uk with: what happened, what you expected, and your device model and Android version.

How do I join a friend's group?

Ask your friend for their 6-character invite code. In the app tap MY GROUPS → Join tab → enter the code → tap Join Group.

Why do I see fewer stars than I remember?

Stars always show your best result for each level. If you replay using more hints, the count won't go down — it stays at your personal best.

How do I delete my account?

Email support@caretrace.uk from your registered address. We will delete all your data within 30 days per our Privacy Policy.
