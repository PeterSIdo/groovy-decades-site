How to Play

Three steps — hours of fun

1
☷

Find the Words

Drag across the letter grid to find all the hidden words — left to right, top to bottom, or diagonally. Each word is from the 1960s–70s era.

2
❓

Answer the Quiz

Once you've found all the words, a trivia question about the era unlocks. Get it right for a 100-point bonus!

3
★

Earn Stars & Points

Find all words without hints for ★★★. Use hints wisely — each one costs 10 points. Climb the leaderboard!

Scoring at a glance

  • 100 pts per word found in the grid
  • +100 pts quiz answer bonus
  • −10 pts per hint used
  • ★★★ No hints used
  • ★★ 1–2 hints
  • ★ 3 or more hints
