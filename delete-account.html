Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go

If you would like to permanently delete your Groovy Decades account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to privacy@caretrace.uk with the subject line "Delete my account". Please include the email address registered to your Groovy Decades account so we can locate it.

What gets deleted

Upon receiving your request we will permanently delete:

  • Your email address and display name
  • All game scores, star ratings, and level progress
  • Your group memberships and leaderboard entries
  • Any challenge records linked to your account

Retention period

Deletion is completed within 30 days of receiving your request. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been removed.

Questions

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at privacy@caretrace.uk.
