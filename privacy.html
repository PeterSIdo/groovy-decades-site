Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Groovy Decades, developed by Peter Sido ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects your personal information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

When you register we collect your email address and a display name. During gameplay we store your scores, star ratings, level completion records, and group memberships. We do not collect your real name, address, phone number, or payment information.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to operate the game: saving progress across devices, showing leaderboards within your group, and enabling the challenge-a-friend feature. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.

3. Data storage and security

Data is stored on a private server protected with HTTPS and hashed passwords. We retain data while your account is active. Deleted accounts are purged within 30 days.

4. Children

This App is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Contact us at sidopeter123@gmail.com if you believe a child has registered and we will delete the account promptly.

5. Third-party services

Google Fonts — loaded at build time; no runtime tracking. YouTube links — optional links open in your browser and are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. No advertising or analytics SDKs are used.

6. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

You may request access to, correction of, portability of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Email sidopeter123@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. UK/EU users may also complain to the ICO.

7. Cookies

The App does not use cookies. This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be noted with a new "last updated" date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Data Controller: Peter Sido — sidopeter123@gmail.com
